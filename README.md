# Doomscroll

**Put a light on it.**

A floating bubble for Android & iOS that watches the scroll: it tracks when you are
*actively* scrolling inside Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, and Facebook — time spent,
what you watched, short summaries of the content — all of it searchable, with a weekly
summary that shows you your week in the light.

The point isn't another screen-time guilt graph. It's awareness: you scroll less when
you can see it.

- Floating bubble / non-intrusive overlay — counts only while you're actually scrolling
- Per-app time tracking across the major social apps
- Content history with summaries, fully searchable
- Weekly summary / reckoning
- Android & iOS

Status: concept. Landing page lives in this repo (`index.html`).
